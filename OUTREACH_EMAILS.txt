(Algebraic Combinatorics, 2025) is the starting point of one of my manuscripts. I would be grateful for your assessment.

You proved without a computer that if 7 divides |Aut| then Aut is cyclic of order 7. My manuscript "A computer-free
proof that a Conway 99-graph has no automorphism of order seven" excludes that remaining case by hand. It starts from
your orbit structure and double-counting identities:
- a rational-representation argument forces the exterior orbit matrix to have zero diagonal;
- a rank-4 Gram compression reduces the problem to twelve vectors in R^4;

calculations of Behbahani and Lam.

computer-assisted, with one validation step still in progress). Combined with the literature, including your result,
